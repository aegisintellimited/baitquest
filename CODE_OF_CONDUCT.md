# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BaitQuest aims to be a welcoming, respectful and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code constructively, not people.
- Accept feedback and differing perspectives in good faith.
- Protect private information.
- Focus on what is best for the community and learners.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Maintainers will review reports fairly and take action appropriate to the circumstances.
